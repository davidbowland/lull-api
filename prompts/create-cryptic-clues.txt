<devices>
Three devices, and nothing else. Every one of them works on a word the SOLVER MUST SUPPLY. That is the point: the letters the wordplay operates on are never printed in the clue, so the solver cannot finish from the wordplay alone and the definition is what settles it.

- "charade": two or more shorter words, joined in order, spell the answer. The clue gives a synonym for each part, in the same order. "Floor covering from vehicle with animal" gives CARPET, from CAR (vehicle) plus PET (animal). Neither CAR nor PET is written in the clue.
- "deletion": one letter is removed from a longer word to leave the answer. The clue gives a synonym for the longer word and an indicator saying which letter goes. "Endless spirit is a mark" gives BRAND, from BRANDY (spirit) less its last letter. BRANDY is not written in the clue. The longer word must be a DIFFERENT WORD, not the answer with a letter stuck on the end -- see the source rules below, which is where clues of this device most often go wrong.
- "doubledefinition": the whole clue is two definitions of the answer, side by side, in two different senses. There is no wordplay and no indicator. "Departed and still remaining" gives LEFT. Keep these SHORT -- two definitions and at most one linking word. Note the joining word: it must be one of the linking words listed below. BUT, THOUGH and YET are NOT on that list and a clue using one is discarded.

USE ALL THREE, AND MIND THE BALANCE. The devices are graded as different difficulties and a batch that is all one device fills one band and ships the day with the other missing:
- EASIER band: "deletion", and "charade" with exactly TWO parts.
- HARDER band: "charade" with THREE OR MORE parts, and "doubledefinition".
At least a third of your clues must come from each band. Clues are discarded individually and often, so a batch that only just clears a third will not clear it after the checks run: aim for half and half.
